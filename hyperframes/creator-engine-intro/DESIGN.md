# CreatorEngine Intro — Visual Identity

## Style Prompt

A technical and rational editorial system on a warm light canvas. The composition should feel like CreatorEngine itself: precise, local, structured, and calm. Use strong black rules, numbered stages, measured spacing, fill-in-the-blank notation, and a single acid yellow-green accent. Every frame should look like a working design instrument rather than an advertisement.

## Colors

- `#F6F5F0` — warm paper canvas
- `#FBFAF6` — raised surface
- `#1D1E1B` — primary ink and structural rules
- `#6D6E68` — secondary text
- `#B9DC42` — single accent for progress, selections, and key ideas

## Typography

- `IBM Plex Sans` — statements, body copy, and interface labels
- `IBM Plex Mono` — numbering, metadata, formulas, and field notation
- Chinese fallback: `Microsoft YaHei UI`, `PingFang SC`, sans-serif

## Motion

- Confident, medium-energy entrances with precise offsets.
- Horizontal push transitions communicate forward reasoning.
- Rules, counters, and progress markers move before supporting copy.
- Motion remains deterministic, measured, and free of decorative bounce.

## What NOT to Do

- No gradients, glassmorphism, neon, or dark tech clichés.
- No decorative illustrations or generated imagery.
- No rounded-card dashboard grid.
- No playful bounce, elastic motion, or random movement.
- No colors outside the defined palette.
